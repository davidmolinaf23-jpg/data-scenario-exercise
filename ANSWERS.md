I would start with the tickets table because it contains several operational signals that could help explain the CSAT decline, including category, priority, response time, status, and reopen behavior.

Beyond the existing tables, I would want to look at issue type, resolution time, transfer history, contact channel, customer segment, reopen reasons, and any recent product, policy, or process changes.

5. Testing a theory

I would compare Billing’s reopen rate over the last three months and against other categories. If Billing’s reopen rate increased at the same time that CSAT declined, it would support the theory and give us an area to investigate further.

Over the following week, I would monitor reopen rate, CSAT, repeat contacts, and QA results to determine whether the change actually improved the customer experience.
